Privacy

This page says what happens to information when you use coach2learn.ca. Tim Girard runs the practice. Coach2Learn is the business name. This is a plain description, not a contract and not a legal opinion.

This website

The pages are static files. There is no account to create here, and Tim has not added an analytics tool or an advertising pixel. The site does not ask you to log in.

Booking with Calendly

The free intro call is booked on Calendly: Intro Call with Tim Girard. It is 30 minutes on Google Meet. Calendly receives what you submit there, such as your name, email address, and the time you pick. Google Meet is how the call itself runs.

Paid sessions are booked the same way, after you pay. Tim sends a Calendly link, and you choose a time. Calendly's own privacy policy covers what you enter on their pages: calendly.com/privacy.

Payment with Stripe

Paid sessions will be taken through Stripe Payment Links. Those links are not connected on this site yet. When they are, you pay on Stripe, not on this website. This site does not see or store your card number. Stripe's privacy policy covers the payment: stripe.com/privacy.

The price shown on the site is the amount charged, in Canadian dollars.

Notes by email, through MailerLite

The signup in the footer is a placeholder. The form action is #mailerlite-signup, and submitting it does not add you to a list or send your address anywhere.

When Tim connects MailerLite, the form will send the email address you submit to MailerLite so he can write to you now and then. You will be able to unsubscribe from any message. MailerLite's privacy policy is at mailerlite.com/legal/privacy-policy. This paragraph will need a small edit once that connection is real, because the form will then send the address.

Fonts

The pages load the typefaces Manrope, Inter, and Newsreader from Google Fonts. Your browser requests those files from Google. That is the font request, not a tracker Tim added. Google's policy is at policies.google.com/privacy.

Asking about your information

To ask what Tim holds about you from this practice, or to ask for a correction, write to [email to be added]. He reads it himself. There is no separate privacy office.

Calendly, Stripe, MailerLite, and Google hold information on their own systems when you use them. A request to Tim covers what he holds. Their policies cover what they hold.
