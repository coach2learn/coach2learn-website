Questions Men Actually Ask

The stuff you're wondering
but might not ask.

Is this therapy?

No. Therapy looks backward to heal. Coaching looks forward to build. If what you're carrying needs a therapist, I'll be straight with you and help you find one. Many men benefit from both.

I'm not a "talk about my feelings" guy.

Good news — this isn't that. Coaching is structured conversation about what you want and what's in the way. Think of it closer to working with a trainer than lying on a couch.

What does it cost?

We cover that in the first conversation once we both know it's a fit. No games — you'll get a straight number, and there's zero obligation to continue after the call.

Do I need to be a dad?

No. I work with men, full stop. But as a father myself, a lot of my work centres on the unique weight dads carry — the pull between providing and being present.

What if it doesn't work for me?

Then we stop. No contracts trapping you, no guilt. Coaching only works if you're getting real value — and you'll know within a few sessions whether you are.
