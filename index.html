Questions Men Actually Ask

The stuff you're wondering
but might not ask.

Is this therapy?

No. Therapy looks backward to heal. Coaching looks forward to build. If what you're carrying needs a therapist, I'll say so and help you find one. Many men benefit from both.

I'm not a "talk about my feelings" guy.

This isn't a couch. It's a structured conversation about what you want and what's in the way. Closer to working with a trainer than to a confession.

What does it cost?

The intro call is free. A 30-minute coaching session is $60 CAD. An hour is $100 CAD. Six one-hour sessions are $500 CAD, to be used within six months — about one session free. The figure on the page is the figure you pay. The breakdown is on Work With Me.

Do I need to be a dad?

No. I work with men, leaders, and professionals. Fatherhood comes up when it's part of your life. Dads who want a free community, rather than coaching, can visit The Dad Pub.

Will you tell me what to do with my money?

No. Money is one of the things men bring to coaching, the way work and relationships do. I'm not a financial adviser, and this isn't financial advice.

What if it doesn't work for me?

Then we stop. Nothing renews on its own, and there is no long contract. The six-session package is six sessions to use within six months. You'll know within a few sessions whether this is useful.

How do I book a paid session?

Pay securely on Stripe. I email you within one business day with a link to choose your session time. For six sessions, that link covers all six, to be used within six months. The only session you can book directly is the free intro call.
