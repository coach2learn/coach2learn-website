Leadership & Team Coaching

Having spent years working alongside leaders and teams through change, uncertainty, and growth, I've seen how the right conversations can shift perspectives, strengthen relationships, and open the door to better ways of working.

Questions You Might Have

A Few Questions
You Might Have.

I've never worked with a coach before. What should I expect?

That's completely okay. You don't need to prepare anything special or know exactly what you want to discuss. Coaching is a collaborative conversation. I'll listen, ask questions, offer observations, and occasionally challenge your thinking to help you explore new perspectives. You bring your experiences and goals. We'll discover the next steps together.

Is coaching only for people facing challenges?

Not at all. Some people come to coaching because they're facing a difficult decision or transition. Others are doing well and simply want to grow, gain perspective, or explore what's next. Coaching can be just as valuable when you're moving toward something as when you're working through something.

Do you coach both individuals and teams?

Absolutely. My work spans personal growth, individual leadership development, and team coaching and facilitation. While the conversations and approaches may differ, the underlying principles remain the same: curiosity, self-awareness, connection, and meaningful progress.

How is coaching different from therapy or consulting?

Coaching is a collaborative process focused on self-awareness, growth, goals, and meaningful change. Rather than prescribing solutions, I help you explore your own perspectives, choices, and possibilities. Consulting typically involves providing specialized advice or recommendations. Therapy can also support personal growth while providing clinical treatment and support for mental health concerns. Each has its place, and coaching can complement other forms of professional support.

What if I'm not sure coaching is right for me?

That's exactly why I offer a free introductory conversation. We can talk about what you're looking for, explore whether coaching might help, and decide together if moving forward makes sense. No obligation. Just a genuine conversation.
